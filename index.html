Our Mission

Three pillars of change

We work across education, health, and skill development — building confident, complete children who will shape a better nation.

Free Education

Daily tutoring, reading programs, and academic support for children who cannot afford private schooling.

Yoga & Self-Defence

Yoga, Judo, Karate, and classical dance training builds discipline, confidence, and physical wellbeing — especially for girls' safety.

Medical camps, health awareness drives, and women's wellbeing programs extending our reach beyond classroom walls.

About Us

Rooted in Ghaziabad,
reaching every child

Founded at 7/56, Chiranjeev Vihar by Dr. Usha Tyagi, Ved Daksha Foundation was born from a single belief: no child should be denied a dignified future because of where they were born. From books and backpacks to yoga performances and community awards — we cover every dimension of a child's growth.

Registered NGO — Ghaziabad, Delhi & Meerut
Free programs: education, yoga, dance, martial arts & health
Annual felicitation with medals, bags & certificates
Covered by local newspapers — Drishti Sagar & others
Book & stationery distribution to meritorious students
Our People

The faces behind the mission

Led by Dr. Usha Tyagi and supported by volunteers, educators, and community leaders who believe every child matters.

Dr. Usha Tyagi
Founder & President
The driving force behind Ved Daksha Foundation. Dr. Usha Tyagi has dedicated years to providing free education and holistic development to underprivileged children of Ghaziabad.
Vishakha Tyagi
Program Head & Anchor
Vishakha leads the on-ground programs and serves as the main event anchor. She coordinates daily classes, events, and volunteer management across all programs.
Dr. Richa Sood
Education Advisor
A respected educationist and author, Dr. Richa Sood advises on curriculum, mentors students, and has been a keynote presence at the foundation's annual events.
Aryan Tyagi
Youth Coordinator
Aryan bridges the gap between the foundation and younger volunteers, organizing youth participation in outreach events and educational programs.
Kavita Singhal
Senior Social Worker
A veteran community worker who extends the foundation's reach into low-income neighbourhoods, organising health camps and women's awareness programs.

What We Do

Programs that change lives

Designed around what our children actually need — not just textbooks, but confidence, health, expression, and joy.

Education

Free Tutoring & Literacy

Daily classes for Class 1–10, covering core subjects and reading skills — completely free, at our Chiranjeev Vihar centre.

Yoga & Fitness

Yoga & Acrobatics

Children train in yoga and acrobatics under expert guidance, performing at annual showcases and competing at district level.

Arts & Culture

Dance & Performing Arts

Classical and contemporary dance training that builds expression, discipline, and pride — showcased at every annual celebration.

Resources

Books & Stationery Drive

Regular distribution of school books, notebooks, and stationery kits to deserving students who cannot afford them.

In the News

Recognised by the community

The annual felicitation saw children showcase talents in dance, song, Sanskrit recitation, and martial arts — a moving display of the foundation's impact.

— Drishti Sagar, Local News Coverage · Ghaziabad

The event was covered by local newspapers and attended by prominent figures including Advocate Rajkumar Gupta, BJP leader Om Rajput, poet Deepak Deep, and social workers Kavita Singhal and Aryan Tyagi.
